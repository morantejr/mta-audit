Methodology caveats

  • Bootstrap intervals describe observed-journey sampling variability, not causal uncertainty.
  • Scenario frequencies describe tested assumptions only.
  • Attribution share is observational credit, not lift.

What these results do not mean

The audit score is not the probability that attribution is correct. No result estimates incremental conversions or causal return.
