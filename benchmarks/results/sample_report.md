## Methodology caveats

- Bootstrap intervals describe sampling variability in the observed journeys, not causal uncertainty.
- Scenario frequencies describe the tested assumptions only.
- Attribution share is allocated observational credit, not lift.

## What these results do not mean

The audit score is not the probability that attribution is correct. No result estimates incremental conversions or causal return.
